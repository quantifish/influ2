# Simulated CPUE data

Simulated catch of lobsters per pot from 2000 to 2017, with changing
seasonal, depth, and soak-time coverage. The teaching scenario
deliberately shifts sampling towards higher-catch months, deeper fishing
during the middle years, and longer soak times in the later years. These
changes can obscure the known annual effect until the relevant
covariates are included. The relationships are specified for
demonstration and are not empirical estimates of lobster catch
relationships. All values are generated by the deterministic simulator
in \`data-raw/compact-brms-fixtures.R\`; no commercial fishing records
are included.

## Usage

``` r
data(lobsters_per_pot)
```

## Format

A data frame with 5,049 simulated pot lifts and five columns:

- lobsters:

  Number of lobsters caught in one pot.

- year:

  Factor identifying fishing year, from 2000 to 2017.

- month:

  Two-digit factor identifying calendar month.

- depth:

  Fishing depth in metres.

- soak:

  Pot soak time in hours.

## Source

Fully synthetic data generated for the package examples.

## Details

The response follows a negative-binomial distribution with a known
log-scale annual effect, seasonal effect, quadratic depth effect, and
linear soak-time effect. Deliberately missing year-month cells and
uneven sampling make the influence and refitted step examples
informative.

The `simulation` attribute records `seed`, `scenario`, and
`year_effect`. The latter is a data frame with columns `year` and
`log_effect`, giving the annual effect used by the simulator before
centring. It is metadata for checking the synthetic example, not an
additional observed covariate. Centre it using the same reference
distribution as the diagnostic before comparing fitted year contrasts.
